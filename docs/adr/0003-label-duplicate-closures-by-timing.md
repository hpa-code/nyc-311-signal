# Label Duplicate Closures by timing, like any other Closure

A Duplicate Closure is a Closure, so it gets its Label from the same 168-hour rule: closed within the Seven-Day Horizon is 0, closed after it is 1 (Delayed). Ticket 10's "kept as 0" means kept rather than excluded. It does not mean forced to 0. Most duplicates close fast: in dev, 258,078 were closed within the horizon and 8,757 after it. Two alternatives were rejected. Forcing every duplicate to 0 would carve an exception into the definition of Delayed, and the model can't see duplicate status at submission anyway. Excluding only the late duplicates would drop positive cases and bias the training data toward 0. HPD's "duplicate of a building-wide condition, original still open" counts too, because a Closure is administrative. The model card reports the duplicate share overall and within each Label.
